## Deliberately not templatized (evidence-driven scope correction)

The approved plan initially called for templatizing all 21 shared families across ts/cs/ng. Lane D's
actual shingle-overlap numbers (max 0.44, most families 0.02–0.25) showed this premise was wrong —
these are independently-authored, language-idiomatic bodies covering the same _topic_, not
near-duplicate text. Per the user's decision mid-audit, only the two families that cleared 0.4
(`rule:code-quality`, `skill:release`) were templatized for real. The other 19 families were left as
hand-authored per-language content — forcing them into slots would have meant either artificial
slot granularity or templates that are 90% empty scaffolding for no real duplication reduction.
